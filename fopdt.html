Parameters & Equations

The FOPDT model and its exact time-domain responses.

Model

Transfer function G(s) = K · e−θs / (τs + 1) K — process gain · τ — time constant (s) · θ — dead time (s).
Differential form τ · dy/dt + y(t) = K · u(t − θ) u is the input, delayed by θ before it affects y.

Exact Responses

Step response (amplitude A) y(t) = 0   (t < θ)
y(t) = K·A·(1 − e−(t−θ)/τ)   (t ≥ θ)
Ramp response (slope A) y(t) = 0   (t < θ)
y(t) = K·A·[(t−θ) − τ(1 − e−(t−θ)/τ)]   (t ≥ θ)
